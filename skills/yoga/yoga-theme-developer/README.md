# theme-developer

Craft yoga teaching narratives, verbal cues, and thematic arcs that connect poses to meaningful intention.

This is a background knowledge skill (`user-invocable: false`). It is loaded automatically by the orchestrator when the storytelling layer of a class is needed.

## What It Does

The theme developer transforms a list of poses into a guided experience. For any theme or sequence, it produces:

- **Core intention** — the single quality or insight the class embodies
- **Verbal anchors** — exact language for opening, recurring phrases, peak moment, and closing integration
- **Pose-by-pose cuing** — specific verbal guidance that weaves the theme through each key pose
- **Teaching notes** — when to speak vs. be silent, tone guidance, level adaptations

Themes are drawn from elemental (earth, water, fire, air, space), seasonal, emotional (letting go, building courage, opening the heart), philosophical (ahimsa, satya, santosha), and anatomical-emotional frameworks.

The theme developer always works in service of the physical sequence — it supports, never fights, the anatomical arc designed by the asana strategist.

## How It Gets Invoked

The orchestrator routes here when a teacher asks:

- "Create a theme around [concept]"
- "What should I say during the peak pose?"
- "How do I weave 'surrender' throughout a 60-minute class?"
- "Give me verbal cues for the backbend section"
- "Develop teaching language for this sequence"
